# Fundraising for Twenty Product Page Reorientation

Status: Active working document  
Iteration: Pilot recruitment page to product page  
Last updated: 17 July 2026

## Purpose

This document is the working space for repositioning fundraisingfortwenty.com
from a time-bounded pilot recruitment page into the authoritative product page
for Fundraising for Twenty.

It supports discussion and claim validation before landing page code or public
copy is changed. Draft wording is directional rather than approved final copy.

## Decision To Date

The page should no longer ask visitors to understand or apply for a free
four-week pilot. It should help a charity understand the app, judge whether it
fits its fundraising work, and take a credible next step towards using it.

The working category statement is:

> Fundraising for Twenty is a fundraising app built on Twenty CRM for small and
> mid-sized charities, developed and supported by 3Trees Digital.

The page should present a real, available product while remaining precise about
which capabilities are established, which are configured during implementation,
and which are still developing.

## Brand Architecture

### Fundraising for Twenty

- The product and primary subject of fundraisingfortwenty.com.
- A specialist fundraising app built on and for Twenty CRM.
- A developed foundation for charity fundraising workflows rather than a blank
  CRM configuration or a generic consultancy engagement.
- The authoritative destination for product capabilities, availability,
  implementation expectations, and product conversion.

### 3Trees Digital

- The company behind Fundraising for Twenty.
- The product developer, Twenty specialist, and implementation partner.
- Responsible for discovery, system and data design, migration, configuration,
  rollout, support, and further product development where relevant.
- Presented visibly on the product site without displacing the product as the
  main brand.

The developing 3Trees Digital website should tell the broader company story and
send fundraising visitors to fundraisingfortwenty.com. The product site should
link back through a concise "Built and supported by 3Trees Digital" treatment.
There is no current need for a separate 3Trees Digital page within the product
site.

### Twenty

- The CRM platform and product foundation.
- Important to category clarity and marketplace credibility.
- Fundraising for Twenty must not be presented as an official Twenty product
  unless that relationship changes and is explicitly verified.

## Audience And Page Job

Primary audiences:

- Small and mid-sized charities looking for a fundraising CRM.
- Charities already considering or using Twenty.
- Visitors arriving from Twenty's app or partner marketplace.
- Twenty team members and ecosystem partners evaluating the product and the
  organisation behind it.

The page should enable a visitor to answer:

1. What is Fundraising for Twenty?
2. Is it relevant to a charity like ours?
3. What fundraising work does it support?
4. What is available now?
5. How would we adopt it and what help is included?
6. Who builds and supports it?
7. What should we do next?

## Positioning Principles

Aim for:

- Product-first language, with implementation as part of successful adoption.
- Concrete fundraising workflows rather than generic CRM promises.
- A clear relationship between the product, 3Trees Digital, and Twenty.
- Availability language that gives visitors confidence they can use the app
  now.
- Honest distinctions between current product capability, implementation work,
  and future development.
- A calm, specialist tone suitable for charity decision-makers and Twenty's
  marketplace team.

The page should not rely on the pilot to create urgency, credibility, or value.
Credibility should come from product specificity, visible workflows, clear
ownership, a practical adoption path, and disciplined claims.

## Product And Availability Model

The current working model is a supported product adoption, not a free trial and
not necessarily a self-serve marketplace installation.

Provisional availability language:

> Available now through supported implementations.

Commercial distinction for this iteration:

> The Fundraising for Twenty app is free for nonprofits. Twenty and any
> implementation or support services are separate.

This needs confirmation against the intended marketplace and commercial model.
Until that is settled, avoid implying that a visitor can install, configure,
and launch the app without 3Trees Digital involvement.

The former pilot may still be useful as evidence of product development or as
an optional early-adopter route. It should not define the product, lead the
page, or remain the main conversion action.

## Claim And Maturity Framework

Every material capability should be classified before final copy is approved.
This gives the page a consistent way to be confident without overstating
maturity.

### Available In The Product

Use direct product language for workflows that exist and can be demonstrated in
the current app. Examples may include donor records, gift records, recurring
giving structures, appeals, grant or opportunity tracking, and imports.

### Delivered Through Implementation

Use implementation language where the underlying capability exists but useful
operation depends on each charity's data model, migration, configuration,
reporting requirements, integrations, or rollout decisions. Dashboards and data
imports are likely to need this treatment.

### In Development Or Scope-Dependent

Name limitations or variability where advanced automation, integrations,
reporting, high-volume processing, or other workflows are not yet repeatably
production-ready. Do not turn roadmap intent into present-tense product claims.

A possible public maturity note is:

> The core fundraising model is available now. Some advanced automation,
> integrations and reporting capabilities are still developing and may be
> scoped as part of an implementation.

This wording remains provisional until the capability review is complete.

## Proposed Message Hierarchy

The primary narrative should be:

`product category -> charity relevance -> workflow evidence -> adoption model -> trust -> next step`

Supporting messages, in priority order:

1. A fundraising app for Twenty CRM.
2. Designed around the work of small and mid-sized charities.
3. Covers the core connected model of donors, gifts, recurring giving, appeals,
   grants or opportunities, imports, and dashboards.
4. Starts from a developed product and is shaped through practical
   implementation.
5. Built and supported by 3Trees Digital.
6. Available now, with some advanced capability still developing.

## Current Structure: Proposed Reorientation

The current visual structure can support the new product story. This iteration
does not require a fundamental redesign.

### Navigation

Keep Fundraising for Twenty as the primary identity. Replace application-led
navigation or actions with product evaluation and adoption language. Add a
clear, restrained route to 3Trees Digital when its site is ready.

### Hero

Current job: recruit organisations into a launch pilot.

New job: identify the product, audience, platform, and adoption path within the
first screen.

Agreed lightweight copy pass:

> Built on Twenty CRM  
> A new fundraising CRM for nonprofits.  
> Give your team the modern tools they need to manage donations and build donor
> relationships you can trust.  
> Available with setup and implementation support from 3Trees Digital.

Primary action: `Tell us about your organisation`  
Secondary action: retain `More details` and its current behaviour.

The headline and supporting paragraph remain unchanged in this pass because
they do not imply a pilot. The right-hand journey retains its existing layout
and is reframed around bringing fundraising into Twenty, starting from a
developed model, and receiving practical implementation support.

### Product Facts Strip

Replace pilot duration, cohort size, and cost with durable product facts:

- Platform: Twenty CRM
- Built by: 3Trees Digital
- Designed for: Small + mid-sized nonprofits
- Available with: Setup + implementation support

Use `nonprofits` in compact product copy for consistency with the existing hero
and the international Twenty marketplace audience. Longer-form copy can use
`charities and nonprofit organisations` where both terms improve clarity.

### Problem And Response

Agreed working direction: explain the trade-off between dedicated fundraising
products and enterprise CRM platforms, then show how Fundraising for Twenty
provides a different starting point.

Left column:

> **The trade-off**  
> **Fundraising systems often make you choose.**  
> Small fundraising teams need software that understands their work, but the
> usual options involve a compromise.  
> **Dedicated fundraising products:** Strong ready-made workflows, but less
> freedom when your organisation needs a different data model or wider CRM.  
> **Enterprise CRM platforms:** Highly configurable, but often complex and
> expensive to implement and maintain.

Right column:

> **A different starting point**  
> **Start with fundraising. Shape the wider CRM.**  
> Fundraising for Twenty combines a developed fundraising model with Twenty's
> flexible CRM foundation.  
> **A developed fundraising core:** Begin with donor, gift, recurring giving,
> appeal and funding opportunity structures already in place.  
> **A wider CRM shaped around your organisation:** Shape the wider CRM's data,
> workflows and relationships around how your organisation works.

This frames the value as a combination of developed fundraising capability,
Twenty's flexibility, and proportionate implementation. It avoids presenting
either dedicated fundraising products or enterprise CRM platforms as
universally unsuitable. The response is intentionally limited to a short
opening and two supporting points so the product-plus-platform logic remains
clear without repeating the proposition.

Design treatment for this iteration: use one continuous editorial field with
near-even columns and a tighter 32-40px desktop gap. The response is
distinguished only through a slight vertical offset, green eyebrow, and
green-accented row markers. Both columns share the same background, headline
scale, and list rhythm. On mobile, they remain straightforward stacked content.

### Workflow Proof

Retain the current card or scenario structure, but describe supportable product
capability rather than completed outcomes or unqualified scale.

Working themes:

- Donors and relationships
- Gifts and imports
- Recurring giving
- Appeals and funds
- Major gifts and grants
- Dashboards and reporting

Each workflow should eventually be paired with current product evidence. Draft
copy should use the maturity classification above before it is promoted to
public copy.

### Process

Replace the four-week pilot process with an adoption journey:

> **Getting started with Fundraising for Twenty**  
> Start with a developed fundraising app, then shape the data, setup and rollout
> around how your organisation works.

1. **Understand your current setup:** Discuss your goals, existing systems,
   fundraising processes, data and wider CRM needs.
2. **Agree the first implementation:** Decide what should be included first,
   how the system should be structured and how existing data will move across.
3. **Prepare the data and team:** Move and validate the agreed data, test the
   setup with real scenarios and prepare the team for day-to-day use.
4. **Launch and improve:** Move into live use with support, then refine the
   system as your organisation's needs evolve.

This should make implementation feel practical and bounded. It should not make
the app sound like an undeveloped custom-build engagement.

### Outcomes

Use this section to explain the product-plus-partner relationship rather than
repeat product capability or pilot decision outputs.

> **Product + implementation**  
> **A developed product, shaped around your organisation.**  
> Fundraising for Twenty provides a strong fundraising starting point. 3Trees
> Digital helps design and implement the wider CRM around how your organisation
> works.

Product anchor:

> **Fundraising for Twenty**  
> **Start with substantial fundraising capability.**  
> The core fundraising model and workflows are already developed on Twenty, so
> you are not starting from a blank CRM.

Partner narrative:

> **3Trees Digital**  
> **Shape the wider system around your organisation.**  
> 3Trees works with your team to understand how the organisation operates and
> decide how Fundraising for Twenty, the wider CRM and connected systems should
> fit together.

Supporting responsibilities:

- **System design:** Decide how fundraising connects with grants, partnerships
  and wider relationships.
- **Data and migration:** Prepare, map and validate the information moving into
  Twenty.
- **Implementation and improvement:** Support setup, rollout, adoption and
  continued development.

Use a shared, asymmetric editorial composition rather than two equal cards. The
compact product side anchors the developed starting point; the wider partner
side carries the system judgment and delivery explanation. A contextual 3Trees
link belongs here but remains inactive until the public company destination is
confirmed.

### FAQ

Use the FAQ to clarify product adoption, wider CRM scope, ownership and the
commercial distinction between the free app and separate platform and service
costs.

Agreed question set:

- What is Fundraising for Twenty?
- Who is it designed for?
- Do we need to use Twenty CRM?
- Is it available to use now?
- Can it be adapted to our organisation?
- Can it support more than fundraising?
- Can you migrate our existing data?
- Who builds and supports Fundraising for Twenty?
- How much does it cost?

Availability wording:

> Yes. Fundraising for Twenty is available now for nonprofits implementing it
> with 3Trees Digital. The core fundraising model and workflows are already
> developed, while migration, integrations, reporting and
> organisation-specific requirements are agreed during implementation.

Pricing wording:

> The Fundraising for Twenty app is free for nonprofits, with no separate
> product or licence fee from 3Trees Digital.
>
> You may still need to pay for your Twenty workspace or hosting. 3Trees charges
> separately for agreed services such as implementation, data migration,
> configuration, integrations and training. Ongoing support or further
> development can also be agreed where needed.

### Final Action

Replace `Apply for the pilot` with a low-pressure invitation to discuss fit and
next steps.

Working territory:

> **Interested?**  
> Tell us what you use today, what is getting in the way and what you need your
> fundraising CRM to support. We'll help you assess whether Fundraising for
> Twenty is the right fit and what a sensible first implementation could look
> like.

Primary action: `Tell us about your organisation`  
Secondary action: `What to expect`

### Enquiry Form And Details Panel

The existing form structure and capture flow remain in place, but the public
surface is an expression of interest rather than a pilot application.

Visible enquiry language:

- Panel title: `Tell us about your organisation`
- Description: `Short enquiry: three sections, about 3-5 minutes.`
- Third-tab expectation: `What happens next`
- Optional field: `Additional context`
- Submission action: `Send enquiry`
- Success state: `Enquiry received`

The `What to expect` panel uses five diligence sections:

1. What you start with
2. Is it a good fit?
3. How getting started works
4. How we work together
5. Costs and current scope

The enquiry and details actions remain intentionally different:

- `Tell us about your organisation` opens the structured enquiry form.
- `Contact us` opens the general contact route for questions that do not need
  the full enquiry form.

Temporary internal compatibility names remain in code and payloads, including
`ApplicationSheetProvider`, `ApplicationForm`, `/api/apply`, `pilotNotes`, and
the `v1` legacy field mapping. These are user-invisible and can be renamed in a
later technical cleanup after the public reorientation is complete.

### Footer And Company Attribution

Working relationship statement:

> Fundraising for Twenty is a specialist fundraising app for Twenty CRM,
> developed and supported by 3Trees Digital. It is not an official Twenty
> product.

Link 3Trees Digital to the company website when its public destination is
ready.

## Pilot-Era Content Treatment

Remove pilot language from:

- the hero and primary proposition;
- the product facts strip;
- the main process section;
- principal calls to action;
- core navigation;
- metadata and social sharing copy;
- application panels or forms presented as the main route.

Retain useful pilot history only where it provides genuine evidence, such as a
product-development note, early-adopter invitation, or future case study. Do
not preserve it merely because the current page was built around it.

## Open Decisions Before Final Copy

### Adoption And Commercial Model

- Can charities buy or begin using the app now?
- Is installation expected through Twenty's marketplace, by 3Trees Digital, or
  through a combined process?
- Is a product walkthrough the right first action?
- Will there be published pricing, implementation pricing, or conversation-led
  scoping?
- Is there still an early-adopter offer, and if so, what role should it play?

### Product Scope

- Which workflows are demonstrably available in the current app?
- Which imports, payment data sources, and integrations are currently
  supported?
- What recurring-giving states and actions are operational today?
- How are appeals, funds, grants, and other opportunities represented?
- Which dashboards or reports are reusable product capability versus
  implementation-specific work?
- Which advanced features require an explicit development caveat?

### Audience And Language

- Use `charities`, `nonprofits`, or a deliberate mix based on the launch market?
- Is `small and mid-sized` specific enough, or should fit be described through
  fundraising complexity, team shape, or income?
- Should the category be `fundraising app`, `fundraising CRM`, or `fundraising
  layer for Twenty` in the hero and marketplace listing?

### Trust And Proof

- Which current screens can be shown publicly?
- Is there a credible product walkthrough or demo environment?
- Can any pilot learning be described without implying customer validation that
  does not yet exist?
- Which claims about Twenty and marketplace availability have been verified?

## Entry Criteria For Implementation

Public copy and code changes should begin after:

1. The product category and adoption model are agreed.
2. The primary conversion action is selected.
3. Current workflows are classified using the maturity framework.
4. Availability, pricing posture, and early-adopter language are confirmed.
5. The canonical relationship wording for Fundraising for Twenty, 3Trees
   Digital, and Twenty is approved.
6. The draft section copy has had a single consistency and claims review.

At that point, the preferred implementation is a focused content revision using
the current page structure, followed by a visual and responsive review.
