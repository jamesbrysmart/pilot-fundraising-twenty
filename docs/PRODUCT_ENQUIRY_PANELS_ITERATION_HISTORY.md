### Can We Trust This?

> This fundraising module is built on Twenty - a modern open-source CRM
> platform. This pilot is a community-built extension, not an official Twenty
> product.

> This is early-stage software. We're being direct about that, and we're
> keeping the pilot group small so support stays responsive and the experience
> stays real.

> The pilot is designed to be safe to evaluate: it runs alongside your current
> system, with a clean stop whenever you choose. No forced cutover during the
> pilot.

> We'll agree the data scope up front and keep access limited to what's needed
> for the pilot.

### Will This Work For Us?

> The fundraising module covers core donor management workflows:

- Donation tracking & history
- Recurring gift management
- Gift batching & processing
- Campaign & fund attribution
- Donor reporting & analytics
- Integration-ready architecture

> These capabilities were designed around real fundraising workflows - unlike
> legacy platforms, the underlying architecture is modern, extensible, and
> doesn't carry decades of technical debt.

> The module is actively evolving. Pilot participants directly shape what gets
> built next based on their actual operational needs.

### Who Is This For?

> This pilot is designed for a specific kind of organization:

- Active fundraising operations: `You run campaigns, track donors, and manage recurring gifts as a core part of your work.`
- Considering CRM migration: `You're planning or actively evaluating a move within the next 12 months.`
- Small-to-mid-sized team: `Lean enough to move quickly, large enough to have real operational complexity.`
- Willing to shape what comes next: `Excited to be part of the next generation of open-source, community-owned nonprofit CRM (this pilot is the start).`

> If your CRM is also a full system of record for volunteers, grants, or
> programs/services, this pilot may not be the right fit yet. That scope is on
> the roadmap, but fundraising is the first module.

### Why Would We Do This?

- Meaningful migration groundwork: `Even if you don't continue, you leave with clearer workflow requirements, data readiness takeaways, and a sharper view of gaps.`
- Clearer fundraising processes: `The workshop and side-by-side trial often surface process improvements you can apply regardless of which CRM you choose.`
- Early access to modern infrastructure: `Evaluate tools built on a modern foundation, without taking on the risk of a forced switch.`
- Be part of what comes next: `Fundraising is the first module. The longer-term goal is a series of nonprofit tools built on open-source foundations, with a community-owned direction over time.`

> The trade is simple: we get real-world feedback from teams running real
> workflows. In exchange, you get a supported, time-bounded evaluation that
> produces clear next-step findings and options. If it's not a fit, you stop
> cleanly. If it is, you can continue without restarting.

### What's Involved?

Timeline (4 weeks):

- `Week 1: planning workshop, agree what to test, and set up with sample data.`
- `Weeks 2-4: run day-to-day fundraising work in the pilot, with short weekly check-ins.`
- `End: review what you learned and decide whether to stop or continue.`

What we need from you:

- `One main contact to coordinate the pilot.`
- `A sample of your data that matches what you want to test.`
- `Willingness to use the system enough to compare it with your current setup.`

What we do:

- `Set up Twenty licenses and configure the fundraising setup.`
- `Run the workshop and keep the plan clear and focused.`
- `Support weekly check-ins and provide a clear end-of-pilot summary.`

After the pilot:

> If you want to continue, we'll discuss next steps, including commercial
> terms, based on what you learned in the pilot.

### Is There A Catch?

- `This is a limited pilot group - we're selecting 5-10 organisations, not opening this to everyone.`
- `The product is early-stage. Some features will be rough. Some workflows will need adjustment.`
- `We're building deliberately, not racing to ship features. Progress is steady but measured.`
- `You'll be working with a small team that's responsive but not a large support organization.`

> There's no hidden agenda. The pilot is free because we want to work closely
> with organisations that can share practical feedback from real fundraising
> work. If it works, there's a path to continue. If it doesn't, you walk away
> with clear findings and no obligation.
